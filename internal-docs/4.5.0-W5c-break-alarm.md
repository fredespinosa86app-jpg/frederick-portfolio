# 4.5.0 W5c: break alarm (browser reminder)

Status: **AUTOMATED_TESTED** (awaiting the owner's live test, then MANUALLY_VERIFIED). Not PRODUCTION_APPROVED.
W5a and W5b were MANUALLY_VERIFIED by the owner on 2026-11-06.

## What it is, in one honest sentence
A **browser reminder**, not a guaranteed alarm: it can only sound while the page is open in a browser. It cannot ring if the browser is closed, the computer is asleep, or the system freezes the tab (QA review R10).

## What it does
- Card **WorkTrack > Alarm (this device)**: Alarm on/off (default **off**), Warn before the limit (0 to 30 minutes, default 5), Repeat while late (off or every 1 to 10 minutes, default every 1), Sound (chime, bell, beep, urgent; default chime), Volume (10 to 100 percent, default 60), Notifications (off; permission asked only when you press the button), Test sound, Silence, Reset clock position. Settings are stored **only on this device** (`localStorage` key `wtAlarm:v1`); unreadable or odd values fall back field by field to the defaults.
- Moments, all from the W5b deadline (database punch time + allowed minutes): a soft tone before the limit; at the limit the chosen sound plays, the tab title alternates "Late: lunch", and the "in" button pulses (W5b); then it repeats, at most **10 times**, then silence.
- It stops at once on: the matching "in" punch, clock out, closing the session, sign-out, switching the alarm off, or **Silence** (Silence applies to that one break; the next break rings again; the late text and button pulse stay).
- A page opened while already late plays **no sound** (flicker and text only). A moment more than 5 seconds old is never replayed (after a reload, sleep or throttled tab).
- `0 = no limit` breaks never sound.
- Changing a setting during a break works the moments out again (past ones are never replayed).
- Sound is made with Web Audio oscillators (10 ms attack, 80 ms release; longest sound 2.4 s; master gain = volume x 0.8). **No audio files.**
- Browsers allow sound only after a click or key press: until then, with the alarm on, the clock shows **"Tap to enable alarm sound"**. Switching the alarm on or pressing Test sound also unlocks it.
- A small same-origin worker file (`scripts/wt-alarm-worker.js`) ticks once a second because page timers are slowed in background tabs. If it cannot start, the page uses its own interval. The CSP already allows same-origin workers (`worker-src 'self'`): **the CSP is unchanged**.
- Notifications (only if you allow them) carry generic text only: "Time to come back from lunch." / "Your lunch ends soon." Never a client, a time or a person.

## Honest limits
- **Real sound, loudness and background-tab behaviour can only be proven on the owner's device.** Tests use a recording stand-in for the audio engine and simulated time.
- Safari, phones and real screen readers are untested. Some browsers (notably phones) pause pages and workers in background tabs; then nothing sounds until you return.
- Notifications depend on the browser and the operating system's focus settings.
- Time is the page's estimate of server time (about 1 second); the deadline itself uses the database punch time.
