# 4.5.0 B3 - Billing due (internal)

Public repository: nothing secret here.

Logic: `scripts/core-schedule.js` (pure; periodAt, periodAfter, isBoundary, dueAt, duePeriods, lastDue, stateOf, listStart, skipPlan). Page: `scripts/owner-due.js`.
Schedule kinds (billing profile `schedKind`): weekly, monthly, midmonth, custom (`anchors` text like "10,25"). Date maths is whole calendar days (no clock); due time = day after last day at genTime in the profile zone (core-time zonedToUtc).
Stored: only `periodSkips/{CLIENTID__YYYYMMDD__YYYYMMDD}` (create-only): clientId, periodStart, periodEnd (YYYYMMDD), reason (3-200), actor, createdAt. Rules: `skipOk` (one profile read via let; step budget checked by `rules-tests/attack/budget_check.py`).
Not stored: periods, states, the first-period choice for a brand-new client (page memory for the visit).
Jumping: the page clicks the Billable hours / Invoices nav button and fills `bhClient/bhStart/bhEnd` or `ivGroup` after those pages have loaded.
Tests: core-schedule.test.mjs, attack 8m, mutations_b2.py (skip rule), qa_b3, emulator periods-b3.test.mjs (demo-periods3).
