# 4.5.0 B2 - approved decisions (record for future reference)

This repository is public. Nothing secret belongs in this file.

Approved by the owner on 2026-11-05 ("B2 prompt approved"), with the recommended defaults below. Source: the B2 prompt and Rev 2 sections 4.4, 4.9, 4.10, 4.11.

## Scope
Invoices from approved billable hours, payments, reversals, credits, due-date changes and adjustments. Owner only, create-only where possible, audited. No email, no PDF, no schedules (B3), no old-page changes.

## Decisions (all "as recommended")
1. **Re-invoicing a cancelled period:** the first invoice keeps the Rev 2 ID `CLIENTID__YYYYMMDD__YYYYMMDD`; a replacement gets `__02`, `__03`, ... A cancelled invoice frees its entries.
2. **Monthly-fee clients:** B2 bills hours x hourly rate only. Clients whose profile arrangement is monthly cannot create an hours invoice yet (fixed-fee invoices: B3 or later).
3. **Balance checks:** the invoice carries a small cached balance (net paid and adjustments). Rules require every payment, reversal and adjustment to be paired with the invoice update in the same batch and not to exceed the balance. The page recomputes the true balance from the records and warns if the cache disagrees.
4. **Reversals:** whole payments only, one reversal per payment (the reversal ID is derived from the payment ID).
5. **Tax, discounts, extra lines:** none. An invoice is hours x rate plus adjustments.
6. **Payment date:** typed by the owner, never in the future.

## Fixed rules from Rev 2
Workflow DRAFT -> READY -> SENT, CANCELLED from DRAFT or READY; payment state UNPAID / PARTIALLY_PAID / PAID and the overdue flag are derived, never typed. Payments are create-only with browser-generated IDs and a payload hash; overpayment is refused unless recorded as a credit with a reason; due date after sending changes only through a new record with a reason; total = rate x minutes / 60 rounded once (half up); currency never changes on an invoice.

## Database changes planned
New: `invoices`, `invoiceAudit`, `payments`, `paymentReversals`, `invoiceAdjustments`, `invoiceDueDates`. B1 entry rule: `relatedInvoiceId` may change only between empty and its invoice ID, in the same batch as the invoice change, minutes untouched. Create-only records join the W3 tamper-evident log.
