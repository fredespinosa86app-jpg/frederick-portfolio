    Services

    Focused support for Amazon sellers and online businesses, from finding sourcing opportunities to documenting every decision.

    Online Arbitrage Research

    Finding Amazon listings that can be sourced from online retailers at a profit, checked against each client’s criteria before anything is passed along.

    Supplier & Sourcing Research

    I research online retail stores and suppliers to find products offered at discounted prices or lower costs than their current selling prices on Amazon. I compare product prices to identify potential sourcing opportunities that meet the client's profit and ROI requirements. I also use AI platforms such as ChatGPT and Claude to help assess the legitimacy and credibility of supplier websites before recommending them as potential sourcing sources.

    Keepa & Sales-Rank Analysis

    Reading price history, sales rank trends, and seller competition in Keepa to judge whether a product sells steadily enough to be worth sourcing.

    Deal Evaluation & Sourcing Criteria

    Screening each lead against clear rules: minimum profit and ROI, maximum number of sellers, estimated monthly sales, and eligibility checks for hazmat, IP, private label, and dangerous goods.

    Deal Sheets & Documentation

    Logging every lead in organized Google Sheets with links, prices, ROI, estimated sales, notes, and screenshots, so each deal can be reviewed and verified.

    AI Workflow Automation

    Using tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Zapier, Make, and n8n to reduce repetitive manual work in research and admin tasks.

    Amazon

    Primary Product Sourcing Platform

    Amazon is one of my primary tools for product sourcing and product research. I use the Amazon marketplace to identify potential profitable products through different sourcing strategies, particularly:

    • Brand Sourcing: Researching products from established brands and identifying potential profitable opportunities.
    • Reverse Sourcing: Starting with potential products or sellers on Amazon and tracing them back to their suppliers or sourcing websites.

    The Amazon marketplace helps me identify potential products for further evaluation using product research tools such as SellerAmp and Keepa.

    • Brand sourcing
    • Reverse sourcing

    Amazon Seller Central

    Seller Account and Product Eligibility Verification

    I use Amazon Seller Central to verify product availability and eligibility within the client's seller account. My primary uses include:

    • Checking whether a researched product is already being sold by the client.
    • Verifying whether a product is eligible for selling in the client's Amazon seller account.
    • Checking gated products that may have potential profitability.
    • Reviewing Amazon's specific ungating requirements to determine what documentation or purchasing requirements are needed to request approval.

    Seller Central helps ensure that product research aligns with the client's existing inventory, account eligibility, and selling requirements.

    Seller Central does not automatically ungate products or guarantee approval. Amazon determines the actual eligibility and approval requirements.

    • Eligibility check
    • Gated products
    • Ungating requirements

    Coupert & Honey

    Used to look for coupon codes and cashback that can lower the buy cost on a sourcing lead, which can improve the estimated ROI.

    • Coupons
    • Cashback
    • Lower buy cost

    Profit, ROI, and sales figures from these tools are estimates, not guarantees. Final sourcing decisions should be confirmed against the client’s own criteria and the seller’s eligibility for each product.
